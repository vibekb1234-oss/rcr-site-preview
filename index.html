Meet RCR

Good people.
Considered craftsmanship.

Roofing, renovations, and restoration. A practical team focused on the details that make your home work better.

FROM UNFINISHED SPACE TO A PLACE TO CALL HOME
Rooney Constructions & Renovation Inc

Built around
your home.

A renovation is personal. It changes the rooms you live in, the way your home feels, and the features you rely on every day.

RCR Construction & Renovations brings together experience in roofing, interior and exterior remodeling, masonry, concrete, and waterproofing. Our work ranges from a specific repair to improvements that connect several parts of a property.

We care about how those parts come together: the preparation behind a finish, the condition beneath a roof, and the details that give a restored chimney or entrance its character. Good craftsmanship begins with understanding what is there and what needs to change.

Serving Calgary, Alberta and Victoria, British Columbia, we welcome homeowners looking for practical advice, thoughtful choices, and a team that pays attention to the work.

HOW TO GET STARTED

A thoughtful start.
A better-informed decision.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Talk through your plans.

Tell us what needs attention, what you want to improve, and how you use your home. Photos and a project address help begin the conversation.

02 / CONSIDER

Make the details clear.

Discuss the proposed work, materials, access, and timing with the team. Ask questions so you understand the scope before moving ahead.

03 / COORDINATE

Agree the next steps.

Bring the priorities together into a practical plan. If your project spans several services, discuss the order of the work and how it connects.

Privacy & accessibility.

A straightforward explanation of this website’s contact experience.

Local demonstration only

This unofficial concept is not operated or approved by RCR. Use fictional details. Forms process entries only in temporary page memory; no data is sent, saved in browser storage or placed in a database. Reloading clears the demo.

External actions

Phone, email, directions and original-site links are genuine external actions only when you choose them. They are separate from the demonstration. External services have their own privacy practices.

Future collection

Any live enquiry, communication permission or booking process requires RCR’s approval and a suitable Canadian privacy and consent review.
